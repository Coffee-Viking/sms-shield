SMS Shield is an Android SMS app focused on local, on-device message filtering.

It supports keyword, number, wildcard, and country-based block and allow rules,
including Unicode message text. Blocked messages are suppressed from regular
notifications and kept in a separate blocked folder.

Messages can be archived, returned to the inbox, deleted, searched, grouped into
conversations, and marked to skip automatic archiving. The app also includes
OTP copy support, local import and export, dual-SIM send options, and light,
dark, and AMOLED themes.

SMS Shield does not use cloud spam detection, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs,
Firebase, Google Play Services, or external AI services.
